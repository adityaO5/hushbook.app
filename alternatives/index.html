What is the best audiobook app alternative?

HushBook is the best overall pick if you want to read along with an audiobook: it highlights every word as the human narrator reads, works on iOS and Android, is free to start, and makes the transcript on your phone. For buying new commercial releases, serialized fiction, self-hosting, or AI voices for documents, use the guide matching that job.

Audible alternatives

5 Best Audible Alternatives in 2026, With Read-Along Ranked First

HushBook is the best overall Audible alternative for listeners who want to read along. It highlights every word as the narrator reads, works with your own files and free LibriVox classics, makes the transcript on your phone, and is free to start. Libby and Hoopla are best for borrowing with a library card, and Libro.fm for buying DRM-free titles. Audible DRM files cannot be imported into HushBook.

BookPlayer alternatives

5 Best BookPlayer Alternatives for Android and iPhone

HushBook is the best overall BookPlayer alternative. It plays the same local files, including M4B with chapters, on both iPhone and Android, and adds what no other player here has: every word highlighted as the narrator reads, transcribed privately on your phone. Voice and Listen are plain Android players, Audiobookshelf suits self-hosted households, and Smart AudioBook Player offers mature Android controls.

Storytel alternatives

5 Best Storytel Alternatives for Reading and Listening

HushBook is the best overall Storytel alternative for people who want to read and listen at the same time. Instead of switching between an ebook and an audiobook, you see each word highlight as the narrator reads. It is free to start, includes free classics, and needs no subscription. Audible is best for buying new releases, Everand for ebook-and-audiobook unlocks, and Spotify for bundled listening hours.

HushBook is the best overall Pocket FM alternative if you want complete books without paying per episode. Speechify alternatives

5 Best Speechify Alternatives for Accessible Reading

HushBook is the best overall Speechify alternative for books. Instead of an AI voice reading text, you hear a real human narrator while every word highlights on screen. It is free to start, has dyslexia, ADHD and low-vision profiles, and transcribes on your phone. If you mainly need PDFs and web pages read aloud, NaturalReader, Voice Dream Reader, ElevenReader and Speech Central are text-to-speech tools like Speechify.
