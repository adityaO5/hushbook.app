# Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-09-13*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** HushBook is a freemium audiobook player that creates private, word-level read-along text for narrated books.

**What it does:** Readers import compatible audiobook files or browse public-domain books from LibriVox and Internet Archive. HushBook transcribes narration on-device, highlights each word in sync, and adds audiobook playback, accessibility profiles, goals, quotes, character maps, and reading analytics.

**Product category:** Audiobook player app; read-along audiobook app; accessible reading app.

**Product type:** Consumer mobile app for iOS and Android.

**Business model:** Free core read-along player and public-domain library. Optional Pro unlocks deeper analytics and other paid features; current price appears in-app.

## Target Audience

**Target users:** Individual audiobook listeners, students, readers with dyslexia, hard-of-hearing listeners, language learners, and people who own DRM-free audiobook files.

**Decision-makers:** Individual user, parent, educator, or accessibility-support professional.

**Primary use case:** Stay focused and understand more by reading synchronized words while hearing original audiobook narration.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Import and play owned audiobook files with audiobook-specific controls.
- Create a private transcript without uploading audio.
- Follow narration word by word and jump by tapping text.
- Build a consistent reading habit with goals, streaks, and analytics.

**Use cases:** Offline travel, difficult-book comprehension, accessible reading, quote capture, language learning, free classics, and local audiobook library playback.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Audiobook listeners lose focus or miss words because narration disappears as soon as it is spoken.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Commercial stores optimize discovery and playback, not private word-level transcripts for owned files.
- Local players preserve position but usually do not create synchronized text.
- Text-to-speech readers replace human narration instead of preserving it.
- Subscription catalogs tie access to plan, region, or listening-hour rules.

**What it costs users:** Rewinding, missed context, unfinished books, weak retention, and recurring catalog fees.

**Emotional tension:** Readers want to finish and understand books without feeling distracted, locked in, or watched.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Word-level synchronized text generated from existing audiobook narration.
- On-device transcription with no audio upload and no account for core use.
- Broad local audio import support and public-domain discovery.
- Vision, dyslexia, and comprehension profiles designed into product defaults.
- Deep reading features: quote cards, goals, streaks, character graph, comprehension checkpoints, and reading analytics.

**How we do it differently:** HushBook starts with a human-narrated audiobook and turns it into an active reading experience on the phone.

**Why that is better:** Readers keep narrator performance while gaining visual context, focus aids, and private transcripts.

**Why customers choose us:** Synchronized text, privacy, owned-file support, accessibility, and a free core experience.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “I need current bestsellers.” | HushBook is not a commercial catalog. Buy or borrow compatible audio legally, then use HushBook for playback and read-along. |
| “Transcription might expose my books.” | HushBook runs transcription on-device after the engine download. No audio upload or account is required for core use. |
| “I only want a simple player.” | A focused local player such as BookPlayer or Voice can fit better when synchronized text and analytics do not matter. |
| “Can I import protected Audible or Storytel downloads?” | Do not assume protected catalog downloads can be imported. HushBook supports ordinary compatible local audio files. |

**Anti-persona:** Someone who only wants a commercial audiobook subscription, requires DRM-store integration, or does not have a supported mobile device.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Subscription fatigue, platform lock-in, missed words, cloud privacy concerns, weak accessibility, or limited local-file support.

**Pull:** Human narration plus synchronized text, no-account core use, on-device processing, accessibility profiles, and free classics.

**Habit:** Existing catalog purchases, playlists, saved progress, and familiar playback controls.

**Anxiety:** File compatibility, transcription time, battery/storage use, and whether protected purchases transfer.
