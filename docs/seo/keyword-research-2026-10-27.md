# HushBook keyword research: 2026-10-27

Research only. No posts written, nothing committed, built or deployed. Method follows the 2026-10-26 plan and the draft notes: Search Console first, then Google autocomplete as the demand proxy, then SERP strength, cannibalization and product fit. Topics already published, drafted (`how-to-get-into-audiobooks`) or rejected in earlier notes are excluded unless the evidence changed.

## Summary

- **Search Console has no unserved cluster.** Every question query with impressions in 16 months already lands on a matching page. The only gaps are tiny and deep: "are audiobooks faster than reading" (2 impr, pos 40), "how big are audiobook files" (1, pos 57), "how many pages per hour audiobook" (1, pos 45), "audiobook library" / "rent audiobooks" (pos 34 to 42). So, as in the last four runs, new topics come from autocomplete plus weak SERPs.
- **The strongest new pattern is "read along with [app]".** The Audible post is the site's biggest impression pool (2,290 impressions over 90 days, 112 phrasings). Autocomplete now shows the same question for Spotify, Apple Books, Kindle, Hoopla and Kobo, and nobody answers the Spotify or Apple versions well.
- **Two product features have no content yet:** the character map and own-file import on iPhone. Both have big autocomplete clusters and weak or vendor-only SERPs.

## Top 5 picks

### 1. How to keep track of characters in a book

- **Why it should win clicks:** a reader problem, not only a listener problem, so the audience is wider than any audiobook query. The phrase is the first suggestion for its seed and spreads into book-specific variants (War and Peace, Game of Thrones, Wheel of Time) and an app query. The SERP is forum threads, a 2016 book-club PDF and a sticky-note brand page. Nobody covers the listening case, where you cannot flip back or see how a name is spelled. HushBook can answer that with two concrete features: the character map (add a character, connect relationships, share the cast summary as text) and the read-along text that shows each name as it is spoken.
- **Target H2s:** "How do you keep track of characters in a book?", "How to keep track of characters when reading a book" (list, family tree, character grid, sticky flags, the edition's character list), "Why is it harder to keep track of characters in an audiobook?", "Is there an app to keep track of book characters?", "How to keep track of characters in War and Peace", "How to keep track of Game of Thrones and Wheel of Time characters". FAQ: "How do you remember characters in a book?"
- **Link to:** `does-reading-along-with-an-audiobook-help`, `how-to-retain-information-from-audiobooks`, `how-to-focus-on-audiobooks`, `is-librivox-safe-and-legal`, `free-read-along-audiobooks`.
- **Watch:** keep it reader-side. "When writing a book" and D&D variants are a different audience.

### 2. Can you read along with Spotify audiobooks?

- **Why it should win clicks:** it copies the site's best impression earner (the Audible post) for the next most-asked service. The SERP has no page that answers it; results are about podcast transcripts. There is a clear, sourced answer: no text read-along for audiobooks, but Page Match (Spotify Newsroom, 2026-02-05: scan a page of a print or e-book and the app finds your place in the audiobook), Recaps (2025-11-13 beta) and Follow-Along (illustrations, not text). That parked "Spotify features change often" risk is handled by citing Spotify's own newsroom pages with dates. HushBook is the natural answer for "I want the words on screen".
- **Target H2s:** "Can you read along with Spotify audiobooks?", "Do Spotify audiobooks have text or transcripts?", "What is Spotify Page Match?", "Do Spotify podcasts have read along?" (yes, creator-enabled transcripts), "How to read along with a Spotify audiobook" (Page Match with your own copy, or a read-along app for files you own), "What are Spotify audiobook Recaps?".
- **Link to:** `can-you-read-along-with-audible`, `can-libby-read-to-you`, `how-to-listen-to-audiobooks-for-free` (its Spotify section links back), `does-reading-along-with-an-audiobook-help`, `/audiobook-with-text`.
- **Verify before writing:** current Spotify support pages for audiobooks and transcripts; region limits of Page Match.

### 3. How to put audiobooks on iPhone

- **Why it should win clicks:** the largest how-to cluster found (41 phrases), with "where do audiobooks go on iPhone" and "how to listen to downloaded audiobooks on iPhone" attached. The site already earns impressions for the M4B slice of it on iPhone (positions 6.7 to 10.3), so Google trusts it on the topic. The SERP is transfer-tool vendors selling software; a free, neutral guide (Finder or iTunes sync, the Files app, AirDrop, iCloud Drive) can beat them. HushBook imports MP3, M4B, AAC, OGG, FLAC and WAV, so the "then play it" step lands naturally.
- **Target H2s:** "How to put audiobooks on iPhone from a PC", "How to put audiobooks on iPhone from a Mac", "How to put audiobooks on iPhone without iTunes", "How to add audiobooks to Apple Books", "Where do audiobooks go on iPhone?", "How to listen to downloaded audiobooks on iPhone", "Why won't my audiobook show up on iPhone?" (DRM: Audible files stay in the Audible app).
- **Link to:** `how-to-play-m4b-files`, `m4b-vs-mp3`, `is-librivox-safe-and-legal`, `free-read-along-audiobooks`, `/m4b-player`.
- **Cannibalization guard:** the M4B post keeps "play M4B on iPhone"; this post owns "put / transfer / add / where". Link the M4B post's iPhone H2 to it.

### 4. How to read classics without getting bored

- **Why it should win clicks:** 32 phrases across "how to read classics", "why are classics hard to read" and "easiest classics to read", with a SERP of small book blogs, Substack and Mumsnet. It was parked on 2026-10-25 for thin evidence; the SERP check confirms nobody else has evidence either, so a post built on the site's existing reading-while-listening research plus concrete tactics (shorter classics first, annotated editions, listening along, character lists) will stand out. HushBook's free catalogue is classics, so this is the most direct route from search to the Discover page.
- **Target H2s:** "Why are classics hard to read?", "How to read classics without getting bored", "What are the easiest classics to read for beginners?", "Are classics better as audiobooks?" (from "classics that are better as audiobooks"), "How to read classics for free", "How to get into classic literature".
- **Link to:** `is-librivox-safe-and-legal`, `free-read-along-audiobooks`, `does-reading-along-with-an-audiobook-help`, `abridged-vs-unabridged-audiobooks`, `audiobook-listening-speed`, and `how-to-get-into-audiobooks` once published. Pick 2 and 8 can then link here.

### 5. Do audiobooks count on Goodreads?

- **Why it should win clicks:** 25 phrases on reading challenges (Goodreads, reading goals, 75 Hard, summer reading, StoryGraph). Seasonal peak in January when challenges reset, so publishing in November or December catches the rise. The SERP is forum threads and opinion pieces; none gives the specific rule for each challenge. 75 Hard has a clear "no audiobooks" rule (per several rules summaries; confirm against the official 75 Hard page). The count-as-reading post answers "is it reading"; this one answers "does it count here", and links there for the research.
- **Target H2s:** "Do audiobooks count on Goodreads?", "Do audiobooks count towards the Goodreads reading challenge?", "How to track audiobooks on Goodreads and StoryGraph", "Do audiobooks count for 75 Hard?", "Do audiobooks count for summer reading challenges?", "Should audiobooks count towards your reading goal?".
- **Link to:** `does-listening-to-audiobooks-count-as-reading`, `how-long-is-an-audiobook`, `abridged-vs-unabridged-audiobooks` ("does an abridged audiobook count?"), `are-audiobooks-good-for-kids` (school and library summer challenges).
- **Verify before writing:** Goodreads help on editions and formats, StoryGraph's audio format and time tracking, the official 75 Hard rules.

**Runners-up worth a slot next:** #6 Apple Books (same sibling pattern as Spotify, smaller cluster) and #7 notes on audiobooks (weak SERP, three HushBook features).
