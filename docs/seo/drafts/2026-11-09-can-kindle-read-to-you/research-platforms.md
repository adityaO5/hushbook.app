## 3. VoiceView screen reader on e-readers

- **What it is and who it is for.** VERIFIED. "Since introducing VoiceView in 2016 to provide screen reader support for blind and low-vision users navigating Kindle devices (now available in English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, and Portuguese)". About Amazon accessibility post, URL in section 2.
- **Bluetooth audio, languages.** VERIFIED. "Pair your supported Bluetooth audio device with your compatible Kindle device to start VoiceView." / "Note: VoiceView over Bluetooth is available in the following languages: English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Spanish." https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TKfsDEx2U1RrGi8o1C (download, 2026-11-09).
- **How to start it (11th gen and newer).** VERIFIED. "Place your Bluetooth audio device into pairing mode. / Press the Power button three times within two seconds to initiate VoiceView. / When you hear audio from your Bluetooth device (within two minutes), hold two fingers spaced apart on the screen to confirm the pairing." Older models: "Press and hold the power button on your Kindle for nine seconds. Then press two fingers spaced apart on the screen for one second." Same URL.
- **Reading a book with it.** VERIFIED. "When you open a book, VoiceView begins reading the book and automatically turns the pages. If the reading stops and you need to start it again, swipe down and to the right with one finger (an "L" shape)." / "Pause VoiceView while reading: Tap on the screen with two fingers at the same time." https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TAy5KCy5PpYSoGtEA3 (download, 2026-11-09).
- **Which devices.** VERIFIED. The identify page lists "VoiceView screen reader over Bluetooth" for the 2026 Kindle, Paperwhite, Paperwhite Signature, Colorsoft, Colorsoft Signature and Kindle Scribe models. It is not listed for the Amazon Kids models or "Kindle (Aluminum - 2026 Release)". Identify page URL in section 2. Writer: say "most current Kindles", not "every Kindle".
- **Language conflict on a product page.** VERIFIED (conflict). The Paperwhite product page says "VoiceView screen reader, available over Bluetooth audio, provides spoken feedback allowing you to navigate your device and read books with text-to-speech (available in English only)." https://www.amazon.com/clp/B0CFPJYX7P (download, 2026-11-09). The help page says six languages. Use the help page or avoid languages.
- **How it differs from Assistive Reader.** Derived from Amazon's pages, not stated as a comparison by Amazon. VoiceView is a gesture-driven screen reader for blind and low-vision users that speaks menus, buttons and the book. Assistive Reader is an on/off reading mode with a player (play, speed, 30-second rewind) and highlighting, and needs Enhanced Typesetting. Present it as "VoiceView reads everything on screen; Assistive Reader reads the book".
- **2016 origin and the Kindle Audio Adapter.** VERIFIED. "Visually impaired customers will be able to use VoiceView for Kindle with the new Kindle Audio Adapter—an Amazon-designed USB audio dongle—to connect headphones or speakers". https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-accessibility-features-for-kindle-e-readers (live, 2026-11-09; published 2016-05-10). Help page: "With VoiceView, blind and visually impaired customers can navigate the device using a screen reader and listen to Kindle books compatible with text to speech using the Kindle Audio Adapter." / "Note: VoiceView does not support music or audiobook files." https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202006400 (live, 2026-11-09).
