## Why this topic

- `scout-german.md`: 145 unique autocomplete suggestions (108 on topic) from 126 calls; sub-intents platform 20, level 20, free 19, short stories/graded readers 12, DW Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten 10, learn 9, kids 5, text/transcript 4. "german audiobooks with text" has the weakest SERP (Learning Ally records, an Audible topic page, Café in Berlin product pages).
- Search Console: no german, deutsch, dw or goethe queries.
- German-specific angle: structure, not speed (German is slower in syllables than English); the verb or separable prefix lands at the end, case endings blur (Hopp 2015). Two advantages: Audible Read & Listen covers German titles, and Deutsche Welle publishes free B1/B2 audio with the full text.
