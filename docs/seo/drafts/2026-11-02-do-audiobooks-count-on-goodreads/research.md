# Research: Do audiobooks count on Goodreads?

All pages fetched 2026-11-03. Goodreads Help, ACX and Renaissance Support (Salesforce) only returned article text to curl with a crawler user agent. "Updated" dates are as shown on each article.

## 1. Goodreads

### 1a. What counts toward the Reading Challenge

- Fact: A book counts if it is on the Read shelf with a read date in the challenge year. Format is never mentioned. Source quote: "All books on your Read shelf with a read date set in the current year are included in this year's Reading Challenge goal." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/000001780 (updated Jul 21, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Did Not Finish books with a current-year date count too. Source quote: "Books on your Did Not Finish shelf with a read date in the current year also count toward your Reading Challenge." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/000001769 (updated Oct 1, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Rereads count once per finish date. Source quotes: "If you've re-read a book, make sure to set individual read dates for each time you finished it." (000001780) and "When you reread a book, you'll add the dates you read the book again." (000001769), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Reread support launched on **February 7, 2017**, not 2016. Source quote: "You don't have to have a start date, but you must have a finish year for the book to count toward your Reading Challenge." https://www.goodreads.com/blog/show/817-rereading-is-here-let-s-say-it-again-rereading-is-here (posted by Cybil, February 7, 2017), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Re-adding the read date puts a removed book back. Source quote: "To add the book back to your reading challenge, remove and re-add the book's read date." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/000001542 (updated Jul 21, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Note: No Help Center page says "audiobooks count" in those words; format is simply not a rule. A volunteer (non-staff) forum answer says it outright: "Manually shelve the audio books. If you add a read date the book will be added to your challenge." https://help.goodreads.com/s/question/0D58V00008H1gj4SAB/can-i-link-my-audible-account-to-goodreads-and-can-i-like-it-to-the-reading-challenge
- UNVERIFIED: Whether marking a book Read today fills in today's date automatically. No official page found.

### 1b. Choosing the audiobook edition and how pages are shown

- Fact: Edition switching (the only live official steps; the old `How-do-I-switch-book-editions` article is 404). Source quote: "iOS app and Android app: Select Book Details, then tap the number of editions. Mobile web and desktop: Under More editions, select Show all [number] editions." then "Select Switch to this edition next to that edition." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/000001803 (updated Oct 1, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: The editions format filter offers "Audiobook", "Audio CD", "Audio Cassette", "Audible Audio" and "MP3 CD" (editions page below), VERIFIED
- Fact: Goodreads librarians put **hours, rounded up, in the pages field** for audiobooks. Source quote: "For audio books, the number of hours rounded up should be used in lieu of number of pages, when that information is known." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/Librarian-Manual-Book-edit-page-number-of-pages (updated Oct 1, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact (example): The *Project Hail Mary* Audible edition (ASIN B08GB58KD5) shows "16 pages, Audible Audio"; the hardcover shows 476. Other Audible editions read "17 pages", "476 pages", or no count at all. https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/56049167-project-hail-mary and https://www.goodreads.com/work/editions/79106958?filter_by_format=Audible+Audio, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- UNVERIFIED: the 16 hrs 10 mins runtime (search snippets only; audible.com returned "not available", then 503).

### 1c. Audible, Kindle and Goodreads

- Fact: Goodreads cannot import from Audible. Source quote: "Note: You can't import from Audible, OverDrive, or Libby. To track books from these services, search for the title on Goodreads and add it to your shelves." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/000001100 (updated Jul 14, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: The only Audible link is one-way, Want to Read into Audible. Source quote: "When you connect your Goodreads and Amazon accounts, books from your Goodreads Want to Read shelf appear directly in a dedicated list in your Audible Library" https://help.audible.com/s/article/view-your-goodreads-want-to-read-list-in-audible (dated Sep 3, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Announced Nov 18, 2025: "This feature is available to some customers now, and rolling out more broadly soon." https://help.goodreads.com/s/announcements/a03ar00000ZzYqFAAV/your-want-to-read-shelf-is-now-in-audible, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Linking syncs Kindle challenge progress but only the Want to Read shelf for Audible. Source quote: "Sync your Kindle and Goodreads reading challenges and Year in Books progress." / "Sync your Want to Read shelf to your Audible app library." / "We only support linking Amazon accounts from the US marketplace." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/000001167 (updated Oct 1, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Automatic counting is Kindle-only. Source quotes: "When you link your Amazon account, Kindle books automatically count toward your reading challenge goal and achievements." (000001780); "Automatically mark books as Currently Reading when you start them and as Read when you finish them in the Kindle app." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/000001371 (updated Jul 16, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED. No page says finished Audible listens are marked Read automatically: Audible is manual.

### 1d. Year in Books

- Fact: Audiobooks count in Year in Books the same as print and ebooks, but pages are taken from the print edition. Source quote: "Audiobooks you complete count toward your Year in Books the same as ebooks and print books. The page count comes from the book's primary print edition (usually hardcover or paperback)." https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/000001520 (updated Sep 4, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED

### 1e. Member poll

- Fact: Member poll (earliest comment May 15, 2018): YES 467 votes (73.3%), Undecided 71 (11.1%), "Who cares. You do you." 70 (11.0%), NO 29 (4.6%); 637 total votes. Top option: "YES, audiobooks ARE just another vehicle for the story". https://www.goodreads.com/poll/show/172864-do-audiobooks-count-on-goodreads-i-know-this-can-be-a-polarizing-topi, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED. Self-selected, not a survey.

## 2. StoryGraph

- Fact: Audiobook tracking shipped June 1, 2022, with pages as the default and minutes as an option. Source quotes: "The default is 'pages'", "Simply choose your audio edition, track your progress, and we'll automatically convert everything to pages for you!", "If you choose to track via minutes, you'll see some new charts", "now you can set an Hours Goal!" https://roadmap.thestorygraph.com/changelog/audiobook-tracking-and-stats- (June 1, 2022), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: You mark an audiobook by switching to the audio edition. Past data can be converted to minutes. Source quote: "if you are switching from print to audio editions and would like them to be tracked as minutes, you'll need to convert them. ... go to Preferences ... and click the link that says 'Convert all data to minutes'." https://thestorygraph.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/79000141927-switching-editions-formats-on-the-storygraph (modified Dec 17, 2025), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: The only import is a Goodreads CSV. There is no Audible or Kindle import. Source quote: "At the moment the only bulk imports we can process are those from Goodreads." https://thestorygraph.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/79000146300-bulk-import-template (modified Apr 2, 2025), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Audible/Kindle sync was requested in 2023; staff (Jan 3, 2023): "We're not sure there's an available API that we'd be able to use given Kindle and Audible are Amazon companies". https://roadmap.thestorygraph.com/requests-ideas/posts/link-audible-kindle-account, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED (still unbuilt is inferred from the import article)
- Fact: A challenge prompt completes once the book has a finish date inside the challenge window. Source quote: "Once the book is marked as 'read' with a finish date that falls within the start and end dates of the challenge, the prompt will automatically register as complete." https://thestorygraph.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/79000142013-getting-started-with-storygraph (modified Dec 18, 2025), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED. Audiobook eligibility in community challenges is set by each host. No platform-wide rule was found.

## 3. Fable

- Fact: Fable says audiobooks count toward its reading goal. Source quote: "Audiobooks are REAL books! Ebooks are REAL books! It is not just physical books that count. All types of books count towards a reading goal." https://fable.co/blog/setting-and-reaching-your-reading-goals (January 1, 2025), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Goal counting is by finish date. Source quote: "To be counted towards your stats (yearly or all-time), a book must have a finished date added." https://help.fable.co/article/125-how-do-i-count-a-book-towards-my-reading-goal (updated Dec 23, 2025), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Everand listening syncs to Fable. Source quote: "Yes, once your accounts are linked, your reading and listening activity on Everand automatically updates in Fable." https://help.fable.co/article/191-do-my-everand-books-and-activity-sync-to-fable (updated May 27, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- UNVERIFIED: Whether Fable tracks audiobooks by time. No help article found.

## 6. Library and school reading programs

- Fact: Beanstack logs audiobook minutes. Source quote: "You can track the minutes spent listening to an audiobook, the pages read of a graphic novel, or the number of picture books read with a child." https://help.beanstack.com/hc/en-us/articles/4425070988567-what-type-of-reading-can-i-log-on-the-mobile-app (January 29, 2026), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Boise Public Library, 2026 summer FAQ. Source quote: "Do audiobooks count as reading? Of course! E-Books, audiobooks, e-audio books ... all books count in Summer Reading." https://www.boisepubliclibrary.org/media/da1btmq2/2026-faqs.pdf, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Andersen Base Library (Beanstack, June 1 to August 9, 2026). Source quote: "WHAT QUALIFIES AS READING? For our summer reading program, we count ALL reading! ... Audiobooks? Yep!" https://andersenairforce.beanstack.org/, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Appomattox Regional Library System, 2026 adult summer reading FAQ. Source quote: "Audiobooks count as books." https://www.arls.org/summer-reading/, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: Accelerated Reader asks students how they read, and "listened" is one of the answers. Source quote: "If necessary, select how you read the book (I listened to the book, I read with someone, or I read by myself)." https://support.renaissance.com/s/article/Taking-an-Accelerated-Reader-Quiz-1752608936799?language=en_US, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- NYPL, Chicago Public Library and Kansas City Public Library: no audiobook rule found, or the page returned 403. Scholastic Summer Reading was not checked. UNVERIFIED.

## 8. Hours to pages

- Fact: ACX gives about 9,300 words per finished hour. Source quote: "On average, most performers narrate about 9,300 words per hour." https://help.acx.com/s/article/producing-and-recording-your-audiobook, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED. **Heads-up:** the URL cited in /blog/how-long-is-an-audiobook (`.../how-long-will-my-narrated-audiobook-be`) now 301-redirects here; the page is titled "Narrate an audiobook" and the old title is only a section heading. The figure is unchanged, but that post's source title is stale.
- Fact: Gatekeeper Press words per page are unchanged: "5" x 8" books: 240-260 words/page", "5.5" x 8.5" books: 300-330 words/page", "6" x 9" books: 360-390 words/page" (based on 11.5 pt body font). https://kb.gatekeeperpress.com/help/how-many-pages-will-my-book-be (last updated 24 Oct, 2025), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Derived: 9,300 words an hour ÷ 250 to 330 words a page = about 28 to 37 pages per listening hour. This is our arithmetic, not a sourced figure.

## 9. Surveys

- Fact: Pew (published Apr 9, 2026; survey Oct 6 to 16, 2025; 8,046 U.S. adults): **26%** of U.S. adults listened to an audiobook in the past 12 months, up from 23% in 2021, 20% in 2019 and 11% in 2011. Survey mode changed from phone to web and phone in 2025. https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/04/09/americans-still-opt-for-print-books-over-digital-or-audio-versions-few-are-in-book-clubs/, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED
- Fact: APA 2026 Consumer Survey (Edison Research at SSRS, February 2026, 1,706 spoken-word listeners 18+; released June 5, 2026). Source quote: "58% of Americans age 18+ have listened to an audiobook - an estimated 157 million people". Sales: "$2.43 billion in 2025, 9% over the previous year." https://www.audiopub.org/surveys, fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED. 58% is ever listened; Pew's 26% is past 12 months. Not comparable.
- Fact: NPR/Ipsos (Feb 21 to 27, 2025; 2,033 adults; ±2.3 points): "two in five Americans and readers (41% each) agree that audiobooks are not a form of reading." and "Sixteen percent of Americans have listened to an audiobook in the past month." https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/most-americans-read-its-not-our-priority-leisure-activity (April 4, 2025), fetched 2026-11-03, VERIFIED. The release defines "readers" as both past month and past year; the methodology says past year.
- YouGov: no direct "do audiobooks count" figure found. UNVERIFIED.
