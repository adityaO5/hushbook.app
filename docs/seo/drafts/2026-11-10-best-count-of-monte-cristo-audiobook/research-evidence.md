# Research evidence: best Count of Monte Cristo audiobook

Post: "Best Audiobook Version of The Count of Monte Cristo: Every Unabridged Edition Compared" (slug `best-count-of-monte-cristo-audiobook`).

All pages below were read on November 10, 2026, unless a row says otherwise. Every value is copied from the page named in its row. "VERIFIED" means the value was read on that primary page today. "UNVERIFIED" means no primary page confirmed it. Where two primary pages disagree, both values are recorded.

How pages were read: curl and a headless Chrome (Playwright, `chromiumSandbox: true`, no headed window). Audible US served product pages only with `?ipRedirectOverride=true&overrideBaseCountry=true` (our network IP otherwise redirects to audible.in); a plain curl to Audible returned HTTP 503 after the first search. Apple Books data came from the iTunes Search API plus each Apple Books page's embedded schema.org data.

Blocked or unreachable today (so availability there is NOT verified): Libro.fm (HTTP 403 to curl, headless Chrome and the fetch tool), Kobo (403), Chirp (403), Spotify (search page renders no results without a logged-in session; album pages return no metadata), Blackstone's own library site blackstonelibrary.com (403), Britannica (Cloudflare challenge; BnF used instead), Naxos FAQ (404), Macmillan and Tantor narrator pages (403 / redirect). Naxos's own product pages also say "Due to copyright, this title is not currently available in your region" for our test location, so a direct Naxos purchase could not be tested.

No search volume numbers are cited anywhere in this file.
